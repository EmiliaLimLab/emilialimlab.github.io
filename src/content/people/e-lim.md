Dr. Emilia Lim is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology at the University of British Columbia, and an Investigator at the Edwin S.H. Leong Centre for Healthy Aging.

She obtained her BSc in Bioinformatics at the University of Alberta, where she completed her thesis on the Human Metabolome Project with Dr. David Wishart. She then pursued her PhD in Bioinformatics under the mentorship of Dr. Marco Marra at UBC and the BC Cancer Genome Sciences Centre, working on epigenomic and transcriptomic biomarker discovery for treatment-refractory hematological and pediatric cancers. Most recently, she completed her postdoctoral training with Dr. Charles Swanton at the Francis Crick Institute and University College London, studying chromosomal instability and how it shapes lung cancer evolution.

Her interest in lung cancer in never-smokers led her to develop a framework for studying environmental impact on cancer initiation, through which she discovered that air pollution triggers lung cancer initiation by promoting clonal expansion of cells harbouring oncogenic mutations that accumulate with aging. Her lab performs multi-omic investigations of how environmental pollutants co-opt molecular mechanisms to shape tissue landscapes into diseased states, with a view towards molecular disease prevention and revealing targets for screening and intervention.
